# 📊 Mobile Sales Dashboard

A dynamic and interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze mobile sales performance, revenue trends, customer purchasing behavior, and product insights through visually engaging reports and KPIs.

---

# 📌 Short Description / Purpose

The Mobile Sales Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence solution designed to help analyze and monitor mobile sales data efficiently. The dashboard provides insights into sales performance, revenue generation, customer transactions, and product trends using interactive visualizations and real-time analytics.

This dashboard is intended for business analysts, sales teams, and decision-makers who want to track performance metrics, identify top-selling products, and make data-driven business decisions.

---

# 🛠️ Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

* 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Main platform used for creating interactive dashboards and visual reports.
* 📂 **Power Query** – Used for data cleaning, transformation, and preprocessing.
* 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Implemented for calculated measures, KPIs, and business logic.
* 📝 **Data Modeling** – Built relationships between datasets to enable cross-filtering and advanced analytics.
* 📈 **Data Visualization Techniques** – Used charts, KPI cards, slicers, and drill-down visuals for interactive reporting.
* 📁 **File Format** – `.pbix` for dashboard development and `.png/.pdf` for dashboard previews and sharing.

---

# 📂 Data Source

**Source:** Mobile sales transaction dataset containing information about:

* Mobile brands and models
* Product categories
* Sales transactions
* Revenue and profit details
* Regional sales performance
* Customer purchasing behavior
* Monthly and yearly sales records

The dataset was structured and transformed using Power Query before building relationships and visualizations inside Power BI.

---

# 🚀 Features / Highlights

## 📌 Business Problem

Mobile retail businesses generate large volumes of sales and transaction data daily. However, analyzing this data manually makes it difficult to identify:

* Top-performing mobile brands and products
* Revenue growth trends
* Customer purchasing behavior
* Regional sales performance
* Monthly and yearly business growth

Without a centralized dashboard, decision-making becomes slow and inefficient.

---

## 🎯 Goal of the Dashboard

The main goal of this dashboard is to:

* Provide an interactive platform for monitoring mobile sales performance
* Enable real-time tracking of revenue, profit, and transactions
* Identify top-selling brands and products
* Analyze customer and regional sales trends
* Support data-driven business decisions through visual analytics

# 📊 Walkthrough of Key Visuals

## 🔹 KPI Cards

Displays important business metrics such as:

* Total Sales
* Total Revenue
* Profit
* Total Transactions
* Number of Products Sold

These KPIs provide quick insights into overall business performance.

---

## 🔹 Monthly & Yearly Sales Trend Analysis

Line and area charts visualize:

* Monthly sales growth
* Yearly revenue performance
* Seasonal sales trends

Helps identify periods of high and low sales activity.

---

## 🔹 Product-wise & Brand-wise Sales Analysis

Bar charts and comparison visuals highlight:

* Top-selling mobile brands
* Best-performing mobile models
* Product category performance

Supports inventory and marketing decisions.

---

## 🔹 Region-wise Sales Performance

Interactive maps and charts compare sales across different:

* Cities
* Regions
* Market segments

Helps identify high-performing sales locations.

---

## 🔹 Transaction Monitoring Dashboard

Tracks:

* Customer purchasing patterns
* Number of transactions
* Revenue generated per transaction

Provides insights into customer behavior and sales efficiency.

---

## 🔹 Interactive Filters & Slicers

Users can dynamically filter reports by:

* Brand
* Product category
* Region
* Time period

Improves report exploration and user experience.
